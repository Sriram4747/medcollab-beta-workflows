# Vocle Privacy Policy (Closed Beta)

**Last updated:** 24 August 2026  
**Contact:** vocle.official@gmail.com  

This document mirrors the in-app Privacy Policy (`LegalCopy.privacyBody`).

Vocle is a closed-beta clinical collaboration app for invited doctors in India.

## Summary

- We collect account, workspace, device (FCM), and support-ticket data needed to run the Pilot.
- We do **not** sell data or use third-party advertising / product-analytics SDKs.
- You remain responsible for hospital policy when sharing clinical information.
- Subprocessors: Railway, MongoDB Atlas, Cloudinary, MSG91, Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Full text is available in the Android app under **Profile → Privacy policy** and on the sign-in screen.
